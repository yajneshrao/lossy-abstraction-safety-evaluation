# Ethics and Responsible Disclosure Note

This project studies AI safety information flow without attempting to operationalize harmful or illegal behavior.

## Scope

The restricted copyright objective is used as a classification target because it provides clear safety attributes. Public prompts do not request instructions for acquiring copyrighted material. The research focuses on abstraction, classification, planning authorization, refinement rules, state consistency, and mitigation.

## Publication choices

The repository intentionally excludes operational details that would turn the evaluation into a practical guide for bypassing safeguards. Exact public prompts are limited to non-operational safety evaluation.

## Claims policy

This project avoids sensational framing. In particular, it does not claim a jailbreak, hidden architecture discovery, or production-system compromise. Results are presented as observations from a simulated architecture evaluated by GPT-6 Astra.

## Researcher responsibility

Anyone extending the work should preserve the same boundary unless they are operating in an authorized controlled environment. Safer extensions include additional domains, additional models, repeated trials, automated scoring, context-retention measurements, and mitigation comparisons.

## Goal

The goal is to improve the design and evaluation of multi-stage AI systems by identifying ways that safety-critical information can be lost across component boundaries and by testing mechanisms that preserve or revalidate that information.
